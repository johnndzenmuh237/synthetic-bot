One loop, running non-stop.

Every trade SyntheticBot takes follows the same disciplined sequence — no improvising, no second-guessing, no missed sessions.

SyntheticBot reads price action and indicators against a fixed set of criteria, watching for setups that match — every hour of every session, five days a week.

When conditions align with the strategy's rules, SyntheticBot opens the position itself. No manual click, no hesitation, no missed entry while you're asleep or away from the desk.
